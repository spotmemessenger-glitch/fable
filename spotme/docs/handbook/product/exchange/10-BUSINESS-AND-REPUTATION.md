# 10 — Business Participation & Reputation

> Reconstruction pending A5 ratification. `[PROPOSED]`.

## 10.1 Business participation

- **Verified businesses** may post **Offers** and respond to **Needs** relevant
  to their category. Verification (a business gate before ACTIVE) establishes a
  verified badge and unlocks business features.
- **Labeled sponsorship only.** A business may pay to **promote** an Offer, but:
  - promoted results occupy a **separate, clearly labeled** slot — never mixed
    into organic ranking score (§04.8);
  - **paid placement never overrides relevance or safety** (constitution);
  - sponsored content is always marked as such (product law).
- Businesses get analytics (views, matches, responses) that are **privacy-
  preserving** — aggregate, no exact user location, no sensitive inference.
- Business Offers still obey the privacy model: they surface approximate seeker
  demand, never a seeker's exact location or identity.

## 10.2 Reputation & trust

- **Reputation is derived and non-sensitive:** completed exchanges, confirmed
  resolutions, verification status, report/fraud history. It feeds the `trust`
  ranking signal (§04) and reach limits (§06).
- **Earned reach:** new/unverified accounts start with limited reach and tighter
  rate limits; positive history raises reputation and reach; confirmed fraud
  lowers both and can lead to a ban (device-set propagated).
- **Explainable trust:** a match rationale can surface "verified" / "N completed
  exchanges" without exposing private data.
- **No dark patterns:** reputation cannot be bought; sponsorship is orthogonal to
  trust and is labeled separately.

## 10.3 Two-sided integrity

- Both sides can rate the outcome (`[PROPOSED]` a simple confirm-resolved +
  optional feedback), gated to real conversations to resist fake reviews.
- Reviews/ratings use **authorized, first-party data only** — Spot Me's own
  signals; no scraping of external platforms (constitution / scope §7.5).

## 10.4 Relationship to the Business Platform

- Exchange business features are the entry wedge into the broader **Business
  Platform** (verified pages, promoted events, reservations, analytics — roadmap
  v2.0 §19), which is **Future Scope** and not activated here. Exchange ships its
  business seam **dark** with the rest of the foundation.
