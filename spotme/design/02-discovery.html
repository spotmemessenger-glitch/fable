What's interactive

  • Drag the distance slider — the list re-sorts, markers outside the radius fade off the map, and the radar rings scale to match. One control, three surfaces.
  • Face markers sit on the street plan the way Uber scatters cars, and lift on hover.
  • The count chip top-left tracks how many people fall inside the current radius.

Held to the inbox theme

  • Same tokens exactly: ink #0f0f10, wells #f1f1f3, hairlines #e6e6ea, blue #007aff. Light only.
  • Same bottom bar and the same five destinations, with Discover active and the Messages badge carried across.
  • Same list grammar — 48px avatar, divider indented to 78px, green presence dot.
  • Same photographs, embedded as data URIs.

Two honest notes

  • The map is drawn, not tiled. Real map tiles need a provider the artifact CSP would block, so this is a hand-built street plan at the right visual weight. In the app this slot takes react-native-maps or Mapbox — the layout does not change.
  • Showing faces on a map is the safety-critical decision in this product. It is the hook, and it is also how someone gets found. The map on/off toggle in your notes is not optional, distance should stay coarse by default, and exact position should never leave the device without an explicit consent step.
