Light mode: yes, and better

  • The dark reference was buying one thing — a signal that you are off-grid. That signal fits in the accent, so the whole second skin is not needed.
  • Blue = internet, green = local radio. One token changes. Surfaces, type, spacing, rows and the bottom bar are byte-identical to the other screens.
  • Light also survives daylight, which is where a mesh feature actually gets used — a festival, a street, a dead-signal building.
  • The scan field keeps the Discovery map's grammar: concentric rings, a centre dot for you, faces as blips. Recognisable, not a new idiom.

What's interactive

  • Connect runs Linking… then settles into a Connected outline chip.
  • Blips drift in and out of range by themselves. A faded blip has left radio range — mesh links are not stable like an internet connection, and the screen should say so rather than imply a fixed list.

Two honest notes

  • "~12 m" is an estimate. BLE distance is inferred from signal strength, and RSSI is wrecked by walls, pockets and bodies. The tilde is doing real work — keep it, and never let anyone navigate by that number.
  • Bluetooth is the heaviest lift on the roadmap. It is a separate native transport from Holepunch (react-native-ble-plx), with its own permissions, background limits and platform differences. This screen is cheap; what sits behind it is not.
